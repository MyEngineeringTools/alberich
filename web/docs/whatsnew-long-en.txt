Modern — details (Web)

Modern is one procedure: a free 26-letter end rotor (non-involutory) and visible filler notches on the sheet (5, 7 or 9). They are not derived from rings and plugs.

Telegram layout: ALBV + 4-letter header + 8-letter message ID + body + 20-letter check group (HKDF-SHA-256 and HMAC-SHA-256). The message key is created automatically. Everyday text goes through Base-26.

The check group is not a second message key. It sits at the end, verifies that the telegram is complete and unaltered, and without it there is no plaintext. The header shown in the UI is the encrypted message key (second group of four), not the ALBV stamp.

When creating a sheet: Modern always uses free permutation. Traditional: Mix or Bruno/Caesar only.

The Greek rotor (Beta/Gamma) always sits in the electrical path. Traditional: it does not step; its ring stays 0. Modern: it steps when the left rotor is at a notch; in Modern its ring setting also applies.

Unchanged: historic Enigma M4, generate/view/print monthly sheets, JSON and QR, courier, networks, emergency wipe.

Modern is an experimental rotor protocol. It is not an AES replacement and has no security proof. Confidentiality remains a rotor cipher.
